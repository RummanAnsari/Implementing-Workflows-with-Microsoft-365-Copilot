# Project Overview: Elevate Workflows with Microsoft 365 Copilot

## Your Role

You are a **Production Manager** at a leading pharmaceutical company with responsibilities that include:

- Optimizing Workflows
- Ensuring Regulatory Compliance
- Fostering Collaboration

---

# The Challenge

You must address a **critical supply chain issue** that threatens production and regulatory compliance.

The supply chain is a complex network of suppliers providing raw materials essential for production.

Your task is to:

1. Identify the issue
2. Create an actionable plan to address the problem
3. Analyze and report findings
4. Present a resolution plan

You will use **Microsoft 365 Copilot** alongside provided project resources to simulate real-world workflows and solve this challenge.

---

# Project Objectives

By completing this project, you will:

## 1. Demonstrate Workflow Efficiency
Leverage Microsoft 365 Copilot to streamline processes and improve operational efficiency.

## 2. Enhance Analytical Skills
Use Copilot in Excel to analyze data, identify patterns, and solve complex supply chain problems.

## 3. Develop Professional Communication
Produce polished reports, summaries, and presentations using Copilot in Word and PowerPoint.

## 4. Improve Collaboration
Utilize Copilot in Teams to coordinate tasks, summarize meetings, and improve cross-functional communication.
